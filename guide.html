How to use

Poker Night Manager Guide

Everything you need to run the game from your iPad, iPhone or PC. Buttons are shown like this.

A game night, in order

  1. Open the app from the home-screen icon (iPad/iPhone) or the chip on your PC desktop.
  2. Set the game on Pool: Standard or High stakes, rebuys on or off.
  3. Sign players in on Players. Seats are drawn for you.
  4. Start the clock on Clock + Seats. Tap 📺 TV for the big screen.
  5. Knockouts: tap Out, then who knocked them out.
  6. End of night: tap End of night: pay out & save and follow the 5 steps.
  7. Back up on Stats. Do this after every game.

Before the game

  • Pool → Standard ($20 / $5 bounty / $2 high hand) or High stakes ($100 / $20 / $5).
  • Settings below that: rebuys on/off, rebuy price, starting chips (8,500), minutes per level, payout table and blinds. Every number can be changed.
  • Rebuys stay open through level 6 (1000/2000). The break counts as level 4.

Signing players in

  • On Players, tap a name, or type a new one and tap Add.
  • The two buttons under the name box set what new players join with: Bounty chip $5 and High hand $2. Turn one off before tapping names to sign people in without it.
  • Change one player later with the buttons under their name.
  • Use full last names so the yearly stats keep each person separate.
  • To remove a name from the list, tap ✏️ Edit list, tap the name, then Yes. Their past results stay in Stats. The ✕ on a player's row only removes them from tonight's game.

The clock

  • ▶ Start / ⏸ Pause, ◀ Level / Level ▶, and −1 min / +1 min.
  • Alarms: a siren at 1:00 left (the screen turns red), a buzzer on every knockout, a chime when blinds go up. Use the test buttons to check your volume.
  • The last level keeps repeating its alarms until the game ends.
  • The top of the tab shows the game type: buy-in / bounty / high hand, for example 20 / 5 / 2.
  • 🔆 Keep screen on (On or Off, for each device) keeps the screen awake while Poker Night is open. The line under it says whether the device allowed it.

Sound only works after you've tapped the screen once. If the device won't keep the screen on, set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.

Knockouts and rebuys

  • On Clock + Seats, you can also tap the player's seat or name under the table.
  • Tap Out next to the player, then tap who knocked them out (or Nobody / unknown). Places and bounty chips update by themselves.
  • A mistake? Tap Undo on that player.
  • ↺ Rebuy appears only for players who are out. They go back to their own seat and can buy a new bounty or high-hand chip at the same time. Their finishing place is freed up.

Bounty chips

  • A chip changes hands only when both players are in the bounty bet. A knocked-out player loses 1 chip, never more.
  • B next to a name shows chips held: B = 1, B3 = 3, B0 = none.
  • To pay out extra chips early, tap 💵 Pay 2 extra chips. The player keeps 1. ↩ undo payout fixes a wrong tap.
  • A player who rebuys without buying a new chip is out of the bounty bet.

Tables and seats

  • Tables balance by themselves after every knockout. Moves are listed as Seat changes on the Clock + Seats and TV screens.
  • Tables: tap a player, then tap another to swap them, or tap Move here on a table. Random redraw reseats everyone.

TV screen

  • Tap 📺 TV at the top. You get a big clock, blinds, payouts and every table, sized to fit the screen.
  • Smart TV or HDMI: open the app on that device and tap TV. iPad: AirPlay or a cable shows the iPad's screen.
  • Leave with ✕ Exit TV in the bottom-right corner.

AirPlay copies the iPad's whole screen. While the TV is showing, use your iPhone to run the game, or exit TV mode for a moment.

Side bets and high hand

  • Extras → Side bets: pick who beat whom and the amount. Net totals show underneath and are saved with the game.
  • The high-hand pot builds as people sign in. It's paid once, at the end of the night. A tie can be split.

Ending the game and splits

  • 🏁 End game is on the Players tab (top card) and the Clock + Seats tab, any time.
  • One player left: it goes straight to the end-of-night steps below.
  • Splitting (a chop): when everyone still playing is in the money, tap 🏁 End game. The prize money for their places is shared evenly, in whole dollars. Change any amount they agreed on; the total must stay the same. Then tap Split & end the game.
  • Split players are recorded as tied for 1st, marked split. Stats count it in the Split column, not as a 1st-place win.
  • Changed their minds? Undo split and keep playing is on the Players tab.
  • More players left than places paid? The app says how many still need to go out before they can split.
  • ✕ Cancel game (next to End game) stops a game with nothing recorded: for an emergency, a game that falls apart, or a practice game. Saved games, stats, names, settings and game files stay.

End of night (5 steps)

  1. High hand: tap the winner (tap two or more to split) and type the hand. Required.
  2. Bounties: check each player's chip count and cash value. Fix a count with − / +.
  3. Places: 1st, 2nd, 3rd… with prize money, from the knockout order.
  4. Side bets: the night's bets and each player's net.
  5. Summary: paid, prize, bounty, high hand and net for everyone. Tap Save game.

Game files

Each game night can be its own named file in a folder, for example 2026-10-14 Poker Night.json. The buttons are at the top of Players.

  • ＋ New game: starts a fresh game from your starting file (fees, blinds, payouts and name list), asks for a file name, then saves it. Saved games and stats are kept.
  • 💾 Save saves to the same file. Save as… saves under a new name or in a new folder.
  • 📂 Open… loads a saved game. The clock opens paused.
  • The card shows the file's name and Saved or Not saved.
  • Starting file: open the Starting file line on that card. Use current settings as the starting file updates it, and ⬇ Save starting file makes a copy to share with another device.

On the PC (Chrome or Edge) you pick the folder in the normal Windows box. On iPhone/iPad, choose Save to Files. Game files move between devices with AirDrop or iCloud Drive.

Backups (important)

Each device keeps its own games. Clearing Safari's data or losing the device loses them, unless you have a backup.

  • Stats → Backup & export → ⬇ Download backup. Save it to Files or email it to yourself.
  • If saving doesn't work, use 📋 Copy as text and paste it into a Note or an email.
  • To move games to another device, or after a reset: 📂 Restore from file or Paste backup text. Your old data is kept, so ↩ Undo last restore can put it back.
  • The backup card turns red when a saved game hasn't been backed up.

Stats and spreadsheets

  • Stats ranks players by net winnings, by year. W-L counts games where a player won any money (prize, bounty or high hand).
  • Tap a player for their history. Tap a game for full results.
  • ⬇ Rankings CSV and ⬇ All game results CSV open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Putting it on your devices

PC: double-click the Poker Night chip on your desktop. The dashboard opens with buttons for the app, TV mode and this guide.

iPhone / iPad (once per device, with Wi-Fi or a signal):

  1. Open Safari and go to pokergame03.github.io/poker-night, or scan the QR code on the PC dashboard with the Camera and tap the link.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add. The $10,000 chip appears on your Home Screen.
  3. From now on, open Poker Night from that chip. It works with no internet.

Always open it from the chip, not from Safari: the chip keeps its own saved games. New versions arrive by themselves the next time you open it with a signal.

More than one device (sync)

Open the online app on each device while signed in to claude.ai. They share one game.

  • One device is the director and makes all the changes. The others are watching: they show the same game live, with the game buttons turned off.
  • The button at the top shows where you stand: 🟢 Director, 🟡 Director · offline (changes send when the signal returns), 🔵 Watching, or ⚪ Watching · last update…
  • To switch directors, tap that button on the other device → Take over as director → Yes. The old director switches to watching.
  • Alarms are on for the director and the TV, and off on watching phones. Change it per device from the same button.
  • Saved games are kept in the cloud and appear on every device. Clear history is turned off while syncing.

The PC's desktop shortcut runs its own copy and doesn't sync. To sync on the PC, use the online link on the dashboard.

If something looks wrong

  • No sound: tap the screen once, check the ringer and volume, and use the test buttons.
  • Places or chips look off: Undo the wrong knockout and enter it again.
  • Self-check: the PC dashboard has a Run self-check button that tests the rules (about 40 checks) without touching your games.
  • Games missing on a device: restore your latest backup on that device.
